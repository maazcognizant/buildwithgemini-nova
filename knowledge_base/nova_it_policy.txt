Version 1.0 — IT Operations Department

SECTION 1: INCIDENT PRIORITY & SLA DEFINITIONS
============================================================

Priority levels are assigned based on business impact and urgency.

P1 - CRITICAL
Definition: Complete loss of service for a critical system, or an issue blocking
an executive, VP, or time-sensitive business event (e.g., board presentation,
client demo, production outage).
Response Time: Immediate acknowledgment (within 5 minutes).
Resolution SLA: 1 hour.
Escalation: Automatically routed to on-call Senior Engineer and IT Duty Manager.

P2 - HIGH
Definition: Significant impact to a department or multiple users, or a single
VIP/executive user experiencing a non-blocking but severe issue.
Response Time: Within 15 minutes.
Resolution SLA: 4 hours.
Escalation: Routed to relevant specialist team (Network, IAM, Endpoint).

P3 - MEDIUM
Definition: Individual user impacted, workaround may exist, not blocking
critical business function.
Response Time: Within 1 hour.
Resolution SLA: 24 hours.

P4 - LOW
Definition: Minor inconvenience, feature request, or non-urgent software/license
request.
Response Time: Within 4 business hours.
Resolution SLA: 72 hours.

Note on VIP escalation: Any ticket involving a VP-level or above employee,
or any issue tagged as blocking a live client-facing or board-facing event,
should be escalated one priority tier above its default classification.

SECTION 2: VPN & REMOTE ACCESS TROUBLESHOOTING
============================================================

Common Issue: VPN connects but corporate resources are unreachable.
Steps:
1. Confirm the VPN client shows "Connected" status, not "Connecting."
2. Check for a DNS suffix mismatch; run ipconfig /all (Windows) or
   scutil --dns (macOS) and confirm corp.internal is listed as a search domain.
3. Restart the VPN client service, not just the app.
4. If using a docking station, disconnect and reconnect the dock's Ethernet
   cable — dock-based NICs occasionally fail to bind after sleep/wake cycles.

Common Issue: Laptop screen goes black or flickers when connected to a
corporate docking station (common with certain dock models and external
monitor configurations).
Steps:
1. Disconnect the display cable from the dock and reconnect directly to the
   laptop to isolate whether the dock or the display driver is at fault.
2. Update or roll back the display/graphics driver — docking station display
   issues are frequently caused by driver mismatches after OS updates.
3. Power-cycle the dock itself (unplug for 10 seconds) — this resolves the
   majority of dock-related black-screen issues.
4. If the issue persists and the user has an imminent meeting or presentation,
   advise immediate fallback: present directly from the laptop screen or via
   a mobile hotspot and a different display adapter while the ticket proceeds
   at elevated priority.
Escalation Note: If this issue affects a user immediately before a
client-facing or board-facing event, classify as P1 and escalate immediately
to the Network Operations on-call engineer, providing an interim workaround
within 5 minutes of ticket creation.

Common Issue: VPN repeatedly drops on macOS.
Steps:
1. Check for a pending macOS update; several recent releases have known VPN
   client compatibility issues.
2. Disable "Wi-Fi Assist" temporarily, which can cause tunnel instability.
3. If on Okta/SAML-based VPN authentication, clear the cached SAML session
   and force re-authentication.
4. Escalate to Network Operations if the drop occurs more than 3 times per
   hour, as this indicates a possible gateway-side issue rather than a
   client-side one.

SECTION 3: IDENTITY, ACCESS & NEW HIRE PROVISIONING
============================================================

New hire access requests must include: employee name, department, manager
name, start date, and role/title.

Standard provisioning includes:
- Okta/SAML single sign-on account
- Corporate email and calendar
- VPN access profile matching department
- Access to department-specific shared drives

Provisioning SLA: New hire accounts must be provisioned no later than the
business day before the employee's start date. Requests submitted with less
than 2 business days' notice should be classified P2 and escalated to the
IAM team for expedited processing.

Role changes (promotions, department transfers) require manager approval
before IAM updates access groups. Access changes are not automatic on
title change; a ticket must be filed.

============================================================
SECTION 4: HARDWARE, DOCKING STATIONS & DISPLAY EQUIPMENT
============================================================

Standard hardware refresh cycle: laptops are refreshed every 3 years;
docking stations and monitors every 5 years.

Docking Station Known Issues:
- Certain dock models are known to cause intermittent black-screen or
  flickering display issues when paired with specific external monitors,
  particularly after firmware or OS updates.
- If a user reports display issues "only when docked," treat this as a
  known hardware compatibility issue rather than a software fault, and
  route to Endpoint Hardware team rather than Network Operations, unless
  the issue is also affecting VPN/network connectivity simultaneously.

Loaner Equipment: Available for P1/P2 hardware failures affecting an
employee's ability to work. Loaner requests should be flagged in the ticket
for same-day dispatch where possible.

============================================================
SECTION 5: SOFTWARE & LICENSE REQUESTS
============================================================

Standard software requests (e.g., design tools, productivity licenses) are
classified P4 by default unless the request blocks an active project
deadline, in which case they may be elevated to P3 with manager
justification noted in the ticket.

License requests require manager approval before provisioning license keys
or enterprise seats.
